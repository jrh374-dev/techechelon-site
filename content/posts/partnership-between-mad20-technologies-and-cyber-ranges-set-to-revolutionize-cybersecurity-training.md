MAD20 Technologies, emerging from MITRE Engenuity’s robust environment, has established itself as a comprehensive provider of cybersecurity training, focusing on the MITRE ATT&CK framework. The collaboration with CYBER RANGES will leverage their industry-leading simulation and emulation-based training to create a dynamic and comprehensive learning experience for those in the cyber defense sector.

The concept of "living certifications" developed by MAD20 is rapidly gaining popularity in the cybersecurity field. This approach ensures that certifications evolve in tandem with the ever-changing cyber threat landscape to provide learners with up-to-date skills essential for navigating today’s complex cyber environments. Rooted in the principles of the MITRE ATT&CK framework, MAD20’s certifications are designed to offer a thorough and practical learning experience for professionals in both enterprise and government sectors.

In the company’s announcement, Reggie Stevens, CEO of MAD20, expressed enthusiasm about the partnership and commitment to living certifications, highlighting their role in revolutionizing cybersecurity education.

“Our focus on fully dynamic and interactive training is no longer simply a concept but a now-tangible pledge to ensure that our learners are always at the forefront of cyber defense,” said Stevens. “In collaboration with industry leaders like CYBER RANGES and MITRE, we are setting a new standard in cybersecurity training with a platform designed to provide practical, real-world knowledge that adapts to the ever-evolving and intensifying cyber threat landscape.”

Al Graziano, CEO of CYBER RANGES, echoed these sentiments, emphasizing the importance of this partnership in building a comprehensive training ecosystem.

“Adding the strength of MAD20’s world-class offering around MITRE ATT&CK to our ever-expanding collection of deep-dive simulation-based experiences and capabilities will enable cyber professionals around the world to build the confidence they need to fight increasingly effective and dangerous threat actors in our world-leading, safe-to-fail cyber range environment,” said Graziano. “As adversaries become stronger, this comprehensive training ecosystem will continue to evolve, ensuring that those who wish to damage our critical infrastructure and tear down societies are never one step ahead.”

MAD20 Technologies, now the official provider of MITRE ATT&CK training and certification, brings a wealth of experience from senior U.S. military members in cyber operations. The MAD20 system incorporates software elements developed by The MITRE Corporation for the U.S. Government, offering cutting-edge training and certification in MITRE ATT&CK.

CYBER RANGES, recognized for its role in cybersecurity workforce development and collaboration with international organizations like the United Nations’ ITU, continues to lead in experiential cybersecurity training. Its global involvement and partnerships reflect its commitment to elevating the standards in cybersecurity training.

Company representatives tell TechEchelon the partnership between MAD20 Technologies and CYBER RANGES represents a significant stride in equipping cybersecurity professionals with the skills and knowledge to effectively counteract and prevent cyber threats in an increasingly digital world.
