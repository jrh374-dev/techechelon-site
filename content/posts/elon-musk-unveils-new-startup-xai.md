Elon Musk, the mastermind behind Tesla, SpaceX, and now Twitter, revealed on Wednesday his latest venture, an AI firm named xAI, with the ambitious mission to "comprehend the fundamental nature of the universe." More details about this venture will be unveiled during a live conversation on Twitter Spaces this Friday, as stated on the company's website.

The team forming xAI comprises veterans from esteemed organizations such as DeepMind, OpenAI, Google Research, Microsoft Research, and Tesla. These team members have an impressive track record, having previously contributed to groundbreaking projects like DeepMind's AlphaCode and OpenAI's advanced language models GPT-3.5 and GPT-4, as highlighted on the company's webpage.
