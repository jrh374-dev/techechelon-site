Daniela Sarbu is a dedicated educational leader, whose profound personal journey inspired the establishment of Waldo International School. With a background rich in both personal experiences and academic credentials, Daniela brings a unique perspective to the world of education. Her initial encounters with the educational systems in Romania and later shifts in her academic pursuits have deeply influenced her approach to education, emphasizing understanding over rote learning. Daniela's vision for Waldo International School stems from a deeply rooted belief that education should be transformative, not transactional.

Under Daniela's leadership, Waldo International School has become a beacon for progressive education, where the focus is on nurturing not just academic abilities but also critical thinking and personal growth. Daniela’s strategies include individualized learning plans, smaller class sizes, and an integration of International Baccalaureate programs, each designed to foster a holistic development of students. As a visionary, Daniela is not only looking to educate but to inspire students to be curious, engaged, and thoughtful individuals.

In an exclusive interview, Daniela shares the inspirational story behind Waldo International School, its distinctive educational approach, and her aspirations for its future. Join us as she elaborates on how the school’s commitment to understanding, critical thinking, and personalized education sets it apart, making it an ideal environment for fostering the leaders of tomorrow.

Q: Can you share the story behind the founding of Waldo International School and what inspired you to start this educational journey?

The story behind the founding of Waldo International School is deeply personal and rooted in my own experiences and values. It all began with the birth of my first son when I encountered the challenge of finding a school that truly resonated with what I envisioned for his education. Despite various options in the area, none quite captured the essence of the educational journey I had experienced in Romania.

My own educational background in Romania played a significant role in shaping my vision for Waldo International School. I firmly believe that education is the most powerful tool we can give to our children. It's not just about imparting knowledge but about fostering understanding and critical thinking. This belief was ignited during my high school years when I faced a pivotal moment that redirected my educational path.

As I prepared for medical school, a humiliating experience in a math class altered my perspective. I found myself embarrassed by a teacher's insistence on memorization rather than understanding the concepts. It was a moment of realization that rote memorization wasn't the path to true learning. I vowed never to allow such an experience to hinder my education again.

Driven by this conviction, I sought out a college that prioritized comprehension over memorization. With determination, I gained admission to the Academy of Economic Studies, which required a thorough understanding of mathematics rather than mere memorization.

This journey, marked by both challenges and epiphanies, laid the foundation for Waldo International School. It's a place where we prioritize understanding over rote memorization, where we empower students to engage critically with the material and cultivate a lifelong love for learning.

In essence, Waldo International School was born out of a desire to create an educational environment where every child can thrive, where learning is not confined to the classroom but extends to a deeper understanding of the world around us.

Q: How does Waldo School's individualized learning approach differ from other educational models, and why do you believe it is important for student development?

Waldo School's individualized learning approach stands out from traditional educational models in several key ways, emphasizing personalized growth and holistic development. Our School customizes learning plans to meet each student's unique needs, interests and learning styles. There is no one size fits all in Waldo School. With this personalized approach we ensure that students are challenged at their appropriate level and they create a strong educational foundation. Students are encouraged to delve deeply into the subjects, develop a through understanding, and apply their knowledge in real world contexts without the need to purely memorize concepts.

Beyond academic achievement, Waldo School is committed to nurturing the whole child – intellectually, emotionally, and socially. Through a variety of enrichment activities, extracurricular programs, and community engagement initiatives, students develop essential life skills, resilience, and a sense of purpose.

I believe that this individualized learning approach is crucial for student development because it recognizes and honors the unique strengths, interests, and learning styles of each student. By providing a supportive and flexible environment where students can pursue their passions, overcome challenges, and take ownership of their learning, Waldo School empowers them to reach their full potential and become lifelong learners and critical thinkers. This personalized approach not only fosters academic success but also cultivates self-confidence, independence, and a love for learning that extends far beyond the classroom.

Q: With a focus on small class sizes and critical research skills, how does Waldo School ensure that each student receives the attention they need to thrive academically?

At Waldo International School education is not one size fits all. Our commitment to small class sizes and the development of critical research skills is central to ensuring that each student receives the personalized attention they need to excel academically and socially. At Waldo School, we recognize the importance of equipping students with the skills they need to navigate an increasingly complex and ever changing world. That's why we place a strong emphasis on developing critical research skills, including information literacy, analytical thinking, and effective communication. Through hands-on projects, inquiry-based learning experiences, and guided research assignments, students learn how to evaluate sources, analyze data, and construct well-reasoned arguments. We aim for our students to develop into confident, independent learners prepared for success in highschool, college and beyond.

Q: Can you elaborate on the significance of the International Baccalaureate programs at Waldo School and how they contribute to a student's holistic development?

The International Baccalaureate (IB) programs offered at Waldo School play a significant role in fostering holistic development among students. The IB programs at Waldo School expose students to a diverse range of cultures, perspectives, and global issues. Through an internationally-focused curriculum, students develop an appreciation for cultural diversity, intercultural understanding, and global citizenship. This exposure helps students develop empathy, respect, and a sense of responsibility towards others, contributing to their holistic development as compassionate and culturally-aware individuals.

The IB curriculum is renowned for its academic rigor, challenging students to think critically, analyze complex problems, and engage in inquiry-based learning. By encouraging students to explore connections across disciplines and apply their knowledge in real-world contexts, the IB programs at Waldo School promote intellectual curiosity, creativity, and a deeper understanding of the world.

The IB programs at Waldo School place a strong emphasis on the holistic development of students, nurturing their social, emotional, and personal growth. Through activities such as community service, leadership opportunities, and reflection exercises, students develop self-awareness, resilience, and a sense of purpose. The IB learner profile, which encompasses attributes such as being caring, reflective, and principled, guides students in becoming well-rounded individuals who are equipped to navigate the complexities of the modern world.

The IB program contributes to a student's holistic development by fostering global awareness, academic excellence, essential skills, personal growth, and preparation for higher education. Through a comprehensive and rigorous curriculum, the IB programs empower students to become principled, open-minded, and engaged learners who are prepared to make a positive impact in an increasingly interconnected world.

Q: The swimming club, facilitated by an on-site saltwater pool, is quite unique. How has this feature been received by the students and parents, and what benefits have you observed?

The on-site saltwater system pool and the accompanying swimming club at Waldo School have been met with tremendous enthusiasm from both students and parents alike.Being the only private school in Jersey City to offer such an amenity, parents view it as a significant advantage. The convenience of having swim classes held within the school premises eliminates the need for additional travel and scheduling logistics, making it easier for families to incorporate swimming into their children's education.This accessibility ensures that more students can benefit from swimming instruction and enjoy the associated health and wellness benefits.

Q: Looking ahead to 2024, what are the key areas of growth and innovation for Waldo School, especially regarding the expansion of the middle school and sports facilities?

As we look ahead to 2024, Waldo School is focused on several key areas of growth and innovation, particularly in expanding the middle school and enhancing our sports facilities. We are looking to growing our school into a much bigger location to host upper elementary and middle school. In addition we are in the process of getting accredited for the International Baccalaureate Middle Years Programme which starts at 6th grade. We have already received the accreditation for the Primary Years Programme since 2016. This program serves ages 3 years old to 5th grade inclusive.

Regarding sports, this year we plan to introduce basketball and volleyball teams - Waldo Owls to start competing in local and regional competitions. In addition we already started preparing for our Waldo Fencing team.

Strengthening our ties with the local community and fostering meaningful partnerships with organizations and stakeholders is a key priority for Waldo School. We envision collaborative initiatives that promote civic engagement, cultural exchange, and service learning opportunities for students, enriching their educational experience and instilling a sense of social responsibility and global citizenship.

Investing in the professional development of our faculty and staff is essential to maintaining the highest standards of excellence in education at Waldo School. We are committed to providing ongoing training, mentorship, and support to empower educators to innovate, collaborate, and continuously improve their teaching practices. By fostering a culture of professional growth and learning, we ensure that our dedicated team remains at the forefront of educational innovation and best practices.

Q: With the ethical integration of Artificial Intelligence into the curriculum, what are your goals for incorporating AI, and how do you envision it enhancing the learning experience for students?

Incorporating Artificial Intelligence into the curriculum at Waldo School represents a significant opportunity to enhance the learning experience for students and staff alike, while also ensuring ethical and responsible use of technology.

One of our primary goals is to promote digital literacy among students and staff. By integrating AI into the curriculum, we aim to equip students with the knowledge and skills they need to navigate an increasingly technology-driven world. This includes understanding the capabilities and limitations of AI, as well as developing critical thinking skills to evaluate and analyze AI-generated content. As we integrate AI into the curriculum, we are committed to ensuring ethical and responsible use of this technology. We have implemented stringent rules and policies to govern the use of AI in the classroom.

AI has the potential to personalize the learning experience for each student based on their individual needs, preferences, and learning styles. By leveraging AI-powered adaptive learning platforms and educational tools, we can tailor instruction to meet the unique needs of every student, providing targeted support and enrichment opportunities as needed.

AI can help educators collect and analyze data more effectively, enabling them to make data-driven decisions to improve teaching and learning outcomes. By leveraging AI analytics tools, educators can gain insights into student performance, identify areas for improvement, and adjust instruction accordingly. This data-driven approach empowers educators to better support student learning and success.
